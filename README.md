## Amazon Sales Analysis

---

## 1. **Project Objective**
The main objective of this project is to analyze Amazon product sales data to understand product distribution, pricing patterns, discounts, and customer ratings. 
The goal was to convert raw e-commerce data into meaningful insights that can help businesses make better pricing, product, and category-level decisions.

---

## 2. **Key Insights**
Some important insights derived from the Power BI dashboard:

- Most products are concentrated in the **₹0 – ₹5,000 price range**, showing a strong focus on budget-friendly items.
- The **average product rating is around 4.1**, indicating generally positive customer feedback.
- Products with ratings between **4.0 and 4.5** have the highest product count.
- Certain categories offer **higher average discounts**, but higher discounts do not always mean better ratings.
- Product ratings are fairly consistent across categories, with only minor variations.

---

## 3. **Tech Stack**
- **Python (Pandas)** – Used for data cleaning, handling missing values, type conversion, and basic EDA.
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Used to perform data preprocessing and export the cleaned dataset.
- **Power BI** – Used to build interactive dashboards and visualizations.
- **Excel / CSV** – Used as the data format for easy integration between tools.

Each tool was chosen to follow a real-world analytics workflow from cleaning → analysis → visualization.

---

## 4. **Dashboard Purpose & Features**
**Business Problem:**  
Raw e-commerce data is difficult to interpret without proper analysis and visualization.

**Goal of the Dashboard:**  
To provide a clear and interactive view of:
- Product distribution across categories
- Pricing and discount patterns
- Customer rating trends
- Overall performance metrics at a glance

**Key Features:**
- KPI cards for total products, average rating, average discount %, and average discounted price
- Category-wise comparison charts
- Rating distribution and trend analysis
- Price range analysis using bins
- Clean and user-friendly layout for quick decision-making

---

## 5. **Dataset**
- Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/karkavelrajaj/amazon-sales-dataset?utm_source=chatgpt.com
- Size: ~1,350 product records
- Data includes:
  - Product details
  - Category information
  - Actual and discounted prices
  - Discount percentage
  - Ratings and rating counts

The dataset was cleaned and processed before being used in Power BI.
